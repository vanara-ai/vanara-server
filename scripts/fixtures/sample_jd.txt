Data Engineer - Senior Level

We are seeking a Senior Data Engineer to join our growing analytics team. You will be responsible for designing, building, and maintaining scalable data pipelines that power our business intelligence platform.

Required Qualifications:
- 5+ years of experience in data engineering or a related field
- Strong proficiency in Python and SQL
- Hands-on experience with cloud platforms (AWS, GCP, or Azure)
- Experience with Apache Airflow for workflow orchestration
- Proficiency with Snowflake or similar cloud data warehouses
- Experience with dbt for data transformations
- Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Engineering, or related field
  OR equivalent practical experience (8+ years)

Preferred Qualifications:
- Experience with Databricks and Apache Spark
- Knowledge of Kafka or other streaming platforms
- Familiarity with Fivetran, Stitch, or similar ELT tools
- Experience with Tableau or Looker for BI
- Master's degree in a quantitative field

Key Responsibilities:
- Design and implement end-to-end data pipelines using Python, SQL, and Airflow
- Build and maintain dimensional models in Snowflake using dbt
- Collaborate with data scientists and analysts to deliver clean, reliable datasets
- Optimize query performance and data warehouse costs
- Mentor junior engineers and establish engineering best practices
- Implement data quality monitoring and alerting

About Us:
We are a fast-growing SaaS company in the fintech industry with 500+ employees. Remote-friendly with hybrid options in NYC and SF. We value ownership, continuous learning, and technical excellence.
